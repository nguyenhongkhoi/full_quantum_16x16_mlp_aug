2. PHAT HIEN TU CODE
a) Co chai thong tin (quan trong nhat): sau moi lan trace, moi node chi con 1 qubit = toi da
   3 so thuc. 8 qubit cuoi la tich cua cac trang thai rieng => chi ~24 so thuc.
   276 dac trung Pauli la ham cua ~24 so do; stage strongly-entangling khong them thong tin
   ve anh. Side tap nang len ~48 so thuc: day la thay doi co gia tri nhat cua ban moi.
b) MLP-256 lon gap 12 lan nhung chi hon ~0,2-0,3 diem => gioi han nam o core, khong o head.
c) Conv va pool cung tac dong len 1 cap qubit => gop lai chi la 1 unitary. Moi block luu
   32 tham so, hieu dung toi da 12. Khong co gate noi qua ranh gioi giua cac cap.
d) Tham so: 87% la co dien (2 readout = 10.804/12.420); core chi 1.600.
e) Chi phi: theo dem phep nhan, stage 8 qubit + 276 phep do chiem >99% tinh toan cua nhanh;
   tree chi ~0,2%. Chua do thoi gian thuc.
f) Khoi tao init_scale=0.01: gate gan identity nen ban dau moi node gan nhu chi giu con trai.
   Nhanh row: 8 pixel con lai nam o cot 0, gan nhu luon den. Adam van thoat duoc, nhung co the
   de lai lech trong so giua cac pixel (chua do).
g) Morton gom vung 2D tot; row chi tron theo chieu doc o block cuoi. Ca hai deu khong overlap,
   nen dich 1 pixel lam doi cach ghep cap.

4. DE XUAT: CAY MORTON BOND 2 QUBIT, BO STAGE 8 QUBIT
Y tuong: moi node giu 2 qubit (15 so thuc) thay vi 1 qubit (3 so thuc). Van mo phong chinh xac
vi cac cay con doc lap.
  16x16 -> RY -> Morton
  Level A: 2 pixel -> 2 qubit, 1 gate (128 node)
  Level B-F: ghep 2 con [a0 a1][b0 b1], gate (a1,b0) TRUOC, roi (a0,a1), (b0,b1), trace a0 va b1
     => 64, 32, 16, 8, 4 node; moi level 3 gate
     Thu tu nay bat buoc: lam nguoc lai thi truoc gate cuoi chi la tich 2 qubit (6 so thuc).
  Doc: 16 o 4x4 (15 Pauli/o) va 4 o 8x8 (15 Pauli/o)
Core: 16 + 5x3x16 = 256 tham so. Tinh toan ~1 trieu phep nhan/anh, so voi ~43 trieu/nhanh hien tai.

Bien the        Head                                                 Tong
B0              Linear(60,10)                                        866
B1              Linear(15,6) dung chung + Linear(156,10)             1.922
B2              Linear(15,6) dung chung + 156->16->10 (LayerNorm)    3.066

Rui ro: trang thai co the tien ve mixed khi len cao lam dac trung nho; kho toi uu hon.
Gia chinh: khoi tao gate can bang (vd sqrt-iSWAP) va chuan hoa dac trung khong tham so.
Neu van underfit: giu 3 qubit moi node (63 so thuc), chi phi ngang stage 8 qubit hien tai.
